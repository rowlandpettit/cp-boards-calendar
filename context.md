# CP Study Context

## Objective

Prepare for the October 19, 2026 CP-only primary examination using daily protected study time, accurate explanations, practical interpretation and spaced recall. The initial schedule reserves 5-7 AM daily. The plan is provisional and should change as actual pace and weak areas become clear.

## Daily Routine

Begin with approximately 25 fresh questions, using CP and relevant shared AP/CP topics. Review wrong and uncertain answers, resolve the most important gaps through targeted reading or teaching, and review a small selection of CP Anki cards. Keep a mixed component rather than leaving whole domains untouched. A familiar audio explanation during easy stationary gym activity can be optional review, but it does not replace focused questions, calculations or image interpretation.

PathDojo is the main bank. Accessible ASCP CP modules or Arinsburg/Jhang questions provide a second perspective. Choose between secondary sources based on explanatory value and time; do not require all of them. PATHPrimer is an optional source for a confusing topic. Use the personal guide as targeted reference, checking unresolved contradictions against authoritative material. Do not publish paid content.

## Coverage

The [2026 ABPath blueprint](https://abpath.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CP_2026.pdf) is approximately 25% hematopathology, 23% transfusion, 23% microbiology, 20% chemistry and 9% management/informatics. Molecular concepts cross these domains. Give transfusion purposeful attention instead of relying on a question bank's natural proportions.

Use the [current content specifications](https://abpath.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Content-Specifications-CP_final_04102026__2_.pdf) for depth: core mastery, advanced-resident competence and superficial familiarity with fellow-level material. The [official schedule](https://abpath.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-ABPath_CP-Exam-Schedule_FINAL.pdf) uses 55 questions per 66-minute block. Complete the [official tutorial and virtual microscopy practice](https://abpath.org/exam-preparation/) before the final week.

## Adaptation

Record actual minutes, fresh questions, uncertain answers, recurring gaps and useful Anki selections privately. Every week, compare the remaining work with actual capacity, then revise upcoming dated overrides. A missed day is information for replanning, not a reason to double the next day's burden. Repeated-question accuracy is not a fresh readiness measure.

Two hours daily is the foundation, not a promise to finish several banks. Start with a realistic target and add separate sessions only after measuring review time. Prioritize retained understanding and broad core coverage over completion percentages. Keep October 17-18 light. Readiness cannot be inferred from the countdown alone.

## Evidence

[ABPath's preparation advice](https://abpath.org/a-few-words-from-abpath-ceo/) recommends reliable question banks, flashcards and mixed-topic practice. [Recent resident accounts](https://www.reddit.com/r/pathology/comments/1w5bghw/apcp_board_study_advice/) differ on the best commercial source and do not establish a pass guarantee. The practical strategy is one main bank, selective secondary explanations, bounded reading and consistent recall.
